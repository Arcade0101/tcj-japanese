にほんごノート 第1回「〜ので」

9月14日の文法。りゆうを ていねいに 言う 言い方を、聞いて、いっしょに 言って、おぼえます。 復習シート · たしかめテスト · キャスト

5分50秒Ben · Nessa · はる · ゆき

1. オープニング

BenHello, and welcome to Episode 1 of the TCJ review study materials. I'm Ben.

NessaAnd I'm Nessa.

BenIn a few minutes you'll be able to phone an office in Japan and say you're running late. Politely. Even if this is the first Japanese you have ever heard.

NessaWe mean that literally. One small word does most of the work.

BenHere it is.

2. きょうの ことば

はるので。

Benので。

BenTwo beats — hard to miss once you know it.

はるので。

NessaIt means "because." But it sits in a different place than in English.

BenWe give the result first — "I'll be late, because there's been an accident."

NessaJapanese flips it. The reason comes first, and "node" sits in the middle, like a hinge.

BenReason. Node. Result.

3. つくって みよう

BenLet's put one together — reason: I have a fever.

はる熱がある。

NessaThat's the fever part.

BenNow hook our word onto the end.

はる熱があるので。

Nessa"Because I have a fever."

BenAnd the result — I'm taking the day off school.

はる学校を休みます。

Nessa"I'm taking the day off school."

BenNow put it all together.

はる熱があるので、学校を休みます。

Nessa"I have a fever, so I'm taking the day off school." There you go — a complete sentence.

4. いっしょに いって みましょう

BenLet's try the first half.

はる熱があるので。

— 3 びょう —

NessaNice. One more time.

はる熱があるので。

— 3 びょう —

BenThere you go. Now the whole sentence.

はる熱があるので、学校を休みます。

— 5 びょう —

NessaOnce more.

はる熱があるので、学校を休みます。

— 5 びょう —

BenKeep that one. Swap the ending and it works for school, for work, almost anything.

5. スキット

はるもしもし、田中さんですか。はるです。おはようございます。

ゆきあ、はるさん。おはようございます。

はるすみません、電車の事故があったので、少し遅れます。

ゆきそうですか。大丈夫ですか。

はるはい。今、新宿にいるので、あと三十分くらいかかります。

ゆきわかりました。事故で電車が止まっているので、みんな遅れていますよ。

はるありがとうございます。申し訳ありません。

ゆき今日は雨なので、道も混んでいます。気をつけてください。

はるはい。あ、それから……実は、まだ熱が下がらないので、今日は早退してもいいですか。

ゆきもちろんです。無理しないでくださいね。

はるありがとうございます。失礼します。

6. わかりましたか

BenFour sentences from that call — let's break them down.

はる電車の事故があったので、少し遅れます。

Nessa"There's been a train accident, so I'll be a little late." Reason, node, result — the same hinge you already know.

BenThat's the sentence to keep.

NessaNext one.

はる今、新宿にいるので、あと三十分くらいかかります。

Ben"I'm in Shinjuku right now, so it'll take another thirty minutes." Same shape again.

はる実は、まだ熱が下がらないので、今日は早退してもいいですか。

Nessa"My fever still hasn't gone down, so would it be all right if I left early?"

BenNotice what she's doing there — she isn't explaining herself, she's asking permission.

7. ひとつだけ きをつけて

BenThere's one exception worth knowing.

NessaMost of the time you just stick node on the end and you're done.

はる熱があるので。

NessaNothing added. "Because I have a fever."

BenBut when the word in front of node is a thing — rain, Sunday, ten o'clock — you slip a small "na" in first.

はる雨なので。

Nessa"Because it's raining."

Benあめだので。

BenNot that one.

Benあめなので。

BenThat one.

はる便利なので。

Nessa"Because it's convenient."

BenThat little "na" is the one to remember.

NessaYou already heard it in the skit.

はる会議は十時からなので。

BenSame "na" — because the meeting is at ten.

8. もう いちど いっしょに

BenLast practice, then we'll hear the whole call again.

NessaThe key sentence first, in pieces.

はる電車の、事故が、あったので、少し、遅れます。

— 6 びょう —

NessaNice. Now at normal speed.

はる電車の事故があったので、少し遅れます。

— 5 びょう —

BenAnd the two with "na". Rain first.

はる雨なので。

— 3 びょう —

NessaThen convenient.

はる便利なので。

— 3 びょう —

BenNow let's hear it in a real conversation — see if you can catch it every time it comes up.

9. スキット（もう一度）

10. きょうの ことば

BenFour words from that call, straight out of your Unit 173 list.

はる具合。

NessaHow your body is doing.

はる具合が悪いです。

Nessa"I don't feel well."

BenNext.

はる顔色。

NessaLiterally face-colour — your complexion.

はる顔色が悪いですね。

Nessa"You look pale." It's what a colleague says when they're worried about you.

BenAnd two more.

はる早退します。

NessaTo leave early, from work or from school.

はる熱が下がります。

NessaA fever goes down. Not gets smaller — it comes down.

11. ひとこと

BenOne last thing about that phone call.

NessaHaru didn't only say she'd be late. Near the end of the call, she used a fuller, more formal apology than the one she opened with.

BenIn a Japanese workplace the apology carries as much weight as the reason. And notice how short she kept the reason. There'd been a train accident. That was all.

NessaShe didn't say which line, or how long she'd been standing on the platform.

BenBecause a long explanation starts to sound like an excuse. Give the reason, apologise, move on.

12. まとめ

BenSo. Reason, node, result. You don't need to get it perfect yet — say it a few more times and it'll come naturally.

NessaAnd if the word in front is a thing, slip in a "na" first.

BenThat's the episode. Next time: "temo" — even if it rains, I'm going.

NessaSee you then. I'm Nessa.

BenAnd I'm Ben. Take care of yourself.
